Welcome to ExamForge

Practice as you study.

ExamForge is a free study platform built for Nigerian students; from Junior Secondary through Senior Secondary, UTME preparation, and university courses. It brings past questions, topic-by-topic quizzes, and study materials together in one place, so you can move from learning a concept to testing yourself on it without switching apps.

What you get

  • Past questions and practice quizzes — for SSCE, UTME, and Nigerian university courses, with instant answers and detailed explanations.
  • Topic-by-topic structure — every course is broken down so you can study exactly what you need, not everything at once.
  • Study materials — formula sheets, summary notes, and reference documents, downloadable for offline reading.
  • Daily Dose — short motivational reads to keep you consistent.
  • Events and mock exams — timed practice under real exam conditions.

Why it exists

Because studying should not require expensive textbooks or unreliable internet. ExamForge works on any phone, in any condition, and always keeps your progress.
