Dalton's Atomic Theory

Dalton's Atomic Theory
  1. All matter consists of tiny particles called atoms.
  2. Atoms of different elements have different properties.
  3. Atoms combine in a single whole number ratio.
  4. Chemical changes taking place in an atom involve joining, separation, or rearrangement of electrons.
  5. Atoms cannot be created nor destroyed.
  6. Atoms of a particular element have identical properties.
Invalidation of Dalton's Theory
The first four postulates remain valid.
The discovery of nuclear fusion and radioactivity proved the last two postulates incorrect.
Atoms can be created or destroyed during nuclear processes.
For example, bombarding Aluminum with high-speed neutrons produces Sodium.
KEY POINT
Postulates 1–4 stand; postulates 5–6 fail because nuclear reactions can create and destroy atoms (e.g., Al + n → Na).

Discovery of the Electron

Cathode Ray Discharge Tube
J.J. Thomson discovered electrons using a cathode ray discharge tube.
The tube contains two metal electrodes sealed at either end.
A high voltage of 10,000 volts is applied, and air is partially evacuated to a low pressure.
At pressures below 10 mmHg, the insulating properties of air break down and it becomes a good conductor.
Properties of Cathode Rays
  1. Deflected by electric and magnetic fields, indicating they are negatively charged particles.
  2. Produce X-rays when they strike a metallic object.
  3. Emanate from the cathode and travel in a straight line.
  4. Possess kinetic energy, indicating they are material objects.
  5. Produce fluorescence when they strike the glass wall of the discharge tube.
Charge-to-Mass Ratio (e/m)
e/m = EB²r
J.J. Thomson used a modified cathode ray tube to determine the ratio.
Where: E = electric field strength, B = magnetic field strength, r = radius of circular path.
The experimental value is 1.76 × 10⁸ C/g.
Electron charge = 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁹ C.
Electron mass = 9.1 × 10⁻²⁸ g.
WORKED EXAMPLE — e/m CALCULATION
Given: e/m = 1.76 × 10⁸ C/g, B = 2.5 T, r = 0.05 m. Calculate E and velocity V.
E = (e/m) × B² × r = (1.76 × 10⁸) × (2.5)² × 5 = 5.5 × 10⁹ V
V = E / B = 5.5 × 10⁹ / 2.5 = 2.2 × 10⁹ cm/s
Millikan Oil Drop Experiment
  1. X-rays knocked electrons from gas molecules in the air.
  2. Oil droplets fell through a hole in the positive plate and picked up electrons.
  3. The mass of the droplet was measured from its rate of fall.
  4. Charges were always whole number multiples of a minimum charge.
  5. This minimum charge is the charge of a single electron.
  6. Calculated mass of an electron = 9.109 × 10⁻²⁸ g.

Proton & Neutron

Discovery of the Proton
Goldstein observed rays passing through a perforated cathode.
These rays are deflected by electric and magnetic fields.
They move towards the cathode, indicating a positive charge.
Rutherford (1919) proved protons exist by bombarding nitrogen and aluminum with alpha particles.
Discovery of the Neutron
Chadwick (1932) discovered neutral particles emitted when elements are bombarded with high-velocity alpha particles.
He named them neutrons.
Neutrons have a mass of 1.0087 amu.

Rutherford's Model & Uncertainty Principle

Rutherford's Atomic Model
  1. Based on the alpha scattering experiment using a thin gold foil.
  2. Most alpha particles passed through, but some deflected.
  3. Electrons move in circular orbits around the nucleus like planets around the sun.
  4. The positive charge of the atom is located in the nucleus.
  5. The atom has a tiny dense central core containing most of its mass, leaving the rest almost empty.
Heisenberg's Uncertainty Principle
Proposed by Werner Heisenberg in 1927.
It is impossible to define both the position and velocity of a subatomic particle simultaneously.
Electrons have dual nature: particulate and wave.
The product of the uncertainty in position and momentum is approximately equal to Planck's constant.
Δx · Δp = h2π   or   Δx · mΔv = h2π
h = Planck's constant = 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ Js.
WORKED EXAMPLE — UNCERTAINTY CALCULATION
Given: Δv = 5.7 × 10⁵ m/s, h = 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ kg m²/s, m = 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ kg. Calculate Δx.
Δx = h / (2π × m × Δv)
= 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ / (2 × 3.142 × 9.1 × 10⁻³¹ × 5.7 × 10⁵)
= 6.6 × 10⁻³⁴ / (3.258 × 10⁻²⁴)
= 2.02 × 10⁻¹⁰ m
